Our Mission

San Joaquin Valley Town Hall is a non-profit organization that is run by an all-volunteer board of directors. Our mission is to bring nationally and internationally renowned, thought-provoking speakers who inform, educate, and entertain our audience! As one or our members told us:

"Each year I give a ticket package to each of our family members. I think of it as the gift of knowledge...and that is priceless."

Featured Speaker

Fall 2026

Andrew Ross Sorkin is a columnist for The New York Times and co-anchor of CNBC's "Squawk Box." He is also the author of the best-selling book "Too Big to Fail" about the 2008 financial crisis.

Lecture day, time, and location

  • All one-day lectures are on the second Wednesday of the month at 10:30 a.m.
  • Lectures are held at the William Saroyan Theatre, 700 M Street, Fresno, CA.
